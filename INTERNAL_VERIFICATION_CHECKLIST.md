## 1. Internal Verification Checklist for Business Owner Sign-Off

### A. Business Identity, Legal Structure & Address
- [ ] **Trading & Legal Name:** Confirm whether the business trades solely as **M-Tech Property Solutions** (or **M-Tech Solutions**) and whether it operates as a sole trader, partnership or limited company (and whether a company registration number or VAT number should appear in the footer).
- [ ] **Contact Person / Ownership:** Confirm the owner/manager's name (the Cylex listing dated 02/09/2025 names *Michael Plav* as the listing submitter) and whether a personal profile or founder introduction should be included.
- [ ] **York Street Address (`York Street, Twickenham, TW1 3JZ`):** Confirm whether York Street is an operating base, correspondence address, or directory profile marker, and whether the full street address or simply *"Based in Twickenham (TW1)"* should be displayed publicly.

### B. Opening Hours & Emergency Availability
- [ ] **Authoritative Opening Hours:** Resolve the difference between the two published schedules:
  - **Yell profile:** `08:00 – 20:30`, Monday to Sunday
  - **Nextdoor profile:** `06:00 – 23:30`, Monday to Sunday
- [ ] **Emergency / Urgent Repairs:** Public profiles and banners mention *"Emergency repairs"* and *"24/7 Emergency & Specialist Repairs"*. Confirm actual emergency call-out hours, typical response times, and whether out-of-hours attendance is offered.

### C. Services, Specialist Trades & Subcontracting
- [ ] **Direct vs. Referred / Subcontracted Work:** Confirm which advertised services are carried out personally in-house and which involve multi-disciplinary team members or vetted trade subcontractors.
- [ ] **Specialist & Regulated Services:** Public profiles mention *Plumbing, Drainage, Drain unblocking, Extractor-fan wiring, Electrical services, Gas services, Extensions, Structural alterations,* and *IT, CCTV & Security*.
  - Confirm the exact scope of plumbing and electrical work undertaken (e.g. minor replacements vs. notifiable Part P electrical work).
  - Confirm whether any Gas Safe, NICEIC, NAPIT or other trade registrations apply (none are claimed on the demo website).
  - Confirm whether gas services or large structural extensions should remain listed or be removed prior to launch.

### D. Insurance, Qualifications & Guarantees
- [ ] **Public Liability Insurance:** Confirm current Public Liability Insurance status and cover level (e.g. £1m / £2m / £5m) so an accurate trust badge can be added once verified.
- [ ] **Workmanship Guarantee / Warranty:** Confirm whether a specific workmanship guarantee period applies to installations and repairs.
- [ ] **Pricing & Quotations:** Confirm whether *"Free Quotes"* applies to all postcodes/job types and whether minimum call-out charges, hourly rates or day rates should be published.

### E. Service Areas & Boundaries
- [ ] **Geographical Coverage:** Confirm the exact service boundary around **Twickenham (`TW1`/`TW2`)** and which specific postcodes or towns across **South West London** and **Surrey** (such as Whitton, Teddington, Hampton, Richmond, Kingston upon Thames, Isleworth) should be listed as primary coverage areas.

### F. Photographs, Project Ownership & Reviews
- [ ] **Photograph Ownership & Permissions:** Confirm that all selected photographs from the Nextdoor business gallery (`https://nextdoor.co.uk/pages/mp-handyman-twickenham-gb-eng`) depict work completed by M-Tech Property Solutions and have client/copyright permission for website publication.
- [ ] **Customer Reviews & Testimonials:**
  - Verify whether additional customer reviews exist on Google Business, Facebook or Instagram (which were restricted from automated retrieval during initial research).
  - Confirm permission to feature the public Nextdoor recommendation (`C. B. — "Polite and speedy responses. Quality work"`) or supply verified customer testimonials with client sign-off.
