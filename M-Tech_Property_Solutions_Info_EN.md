# M-Tech Property Solutions --- Business Information

## 1. Business Overview

**M-Tech Property Solutions** is a property maintenance and
home-improvement business listed in Twickenham, United Kingdom. Public
profiles associate it with handyman work, property maintenance and
general contracting.

The public research identifies the following characteristics:

-   A broad range of advertised services, from small household repairs
    and installations to larger property-improvement and renovation
    work.
-   Residential, outdoor and garden work, alongside property-maintenance
    and commercial services.
-   Publicly stated coverage of **South West London and Surrey** in a
    Nextdoor promotional post. Twickenham is the principal location
    consistently associated with the business.
-   Public profiles on Nextdoor, Yell and Cylex.
-   A telephone number and email address that match between the Nextdoor
    and Yell profiles.

The research did not locate an independent official website. This
records the search result only; it does not establish conclusively that
the business has never had or does not currently have a website.

## 13. Source Limitations and Evidence Notes

This document preserves information reported from publicly viewable
sources in the research dated **20 November 2026**. The source report
states that the following matters were not conclusively established from
public information:

-   The legal status of the business and whether it uses another
    registered or legal name.
-   Whether the York Street address is a customer-facing premises or
    only a profile address.
-   Whether an independent website or domain exists.
-   The current and authoritative opening hours, given the difference
    between Yell and Nextdoor.
-   The exact geographical service boundary beyond the published
    Twickenham association and the Nextdoor South West London and Surrey
    claim.
-   Whether work is carried out personally, by an employed team or
    through subcontractors.
-   Which listed services are carried out directly and which, if any,
    are referred or subcontracted.
-   Qualifications, professional registrations and authorisations for
    specialist electrical, gas, plumbing, drainage, wiring, extension or
    structural work.
-   Insurance status.
-   Ownership and permission to reuse profile photographs and
    confirmation that all images depict the business's own work.
-   The details of pricing, quotation terms and any call-out charges.
-   Whether any guarantee, complaints process or cancellation terms are
    offered.

These limitations are included to preserve the distinction between
information appearing in public-source research and facts that the
research did not establish. They do not negate the fact that the
relevant details and service claims were documented in the cited public
profiles or posts.
